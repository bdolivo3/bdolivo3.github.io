Privacy policy.

Last updated: October 22, 2026.

Who is responsible

Selvativo is a collection of children's books by Bruno D'Olivo Bayerri. For anything about this policy, write to contacto@selvativo.com.

The essentials

The selvativo.com website doesn't ask for your personal data or store it. There's no sign-up, no forms, no newsletter, no advertising and no purchases on the site. We don't use cookies or analytics tools either.

The website, selvativo.com

  • Hosting. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like any server, GitHub receives your IP address in order to show you the page and may keep it for a while in its security logs. It does so under its own privacy statement. We have no access to those logs.
  • Cookies. The website doesn't set cookies. While you have the tab open, it only stores in your browser which book you got, so that it doesn't change as you move between pages. This is explained in the cookie policy.
  • Links to other websites. Some buttons take you to Amazon, to buy the books, and to our Instagram. When you tap them, you leave selvativo.com and those websites' privacy and cookie policies apply.

Children

Selvativo is made for children aged 6 and up, together with an adult. Since we don't collect anyone's data, we don't collect children's data either. If you think any data from a child has reached us, write to us at contacto@selvativo.com and we'll delete it.

If you write to us

If you send us an email, we'll use your address and what you tell us only to reply to you. We'll keep it for as long as the conversation lasts and won't give it to anyone. The legal basis is your consent when you write to us.

Your rights

You can ask us to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict its use, object to its use or take it with you, by writing to contacto@selvativo.com. If you think we haven't handled your request properly, you can file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (www.aepd.es).

Changes

If anything changes, such as the website starting to collect any data, we'll update this page and the date above before it happens. This English version is a translation; if there is any difference, the Spanish version prevails.
