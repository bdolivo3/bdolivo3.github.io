A fact card and a coloring page from the book.

Twelve animals. Twelve fact cards. Only one champion.

A coloring book that's also a game. Every animal of Antarctica has its fact card, with everything there is to know about it; its full-page portrait, to see it whole and in its home; and right across from it, its drawing, waiting for your colors.

And once you've finished coloring it in, its fact card joins the game: attack, defense, speed and life. Pick your animal, find a rival and may the best one win. The rules are at the front, with a worked example, and games are scored right in the book: no loose pieces to lose.

THE ANTARCTIC TWELVE

Emperor penguin · Adélie penguin · Gentoo penguin · Chinstrap penguin · King penguin · Snow petrel · Antarctic petrel · South polar skua · Antarctic shag · Snowy sheathbill · Wandering albatross · Antarctic tern

WHAT'S INSIDE

  • 12 animals to color, each on a full page.
  • 12 full-page portraits, each one facing its drawing.
  • 12 fact cards with real facts: weight, size, habitat, diet, three fun facts, a “Did you know…?” and how endangered the species is according to the IUCN.
  • Duels and a big battle with the cards: one on one, or six against six in teams. Four pages of duel arenas and four big-battle sheets to score games in pencil, erase and play again.
  • Map of Antarctica across two pages, with each animal in its place and the same number as its fact card.
  • Maze, word search and crossword, with the answers at the back.
  • Ice records: the one that keeps its egg warm standing up at forty below, the one with the longest wings in the world, the one that walks about thirty miles to eat… and the strongest, which is settled by playing.
  • Test yourself: six questions, the odd one out and “Guess who I am”, with every answer on the fact cards.
  • Explorer's log: a box and a date for every animal you finish.
  • Caring for the ice: what these animals need and what a child can do without leaving their own neighborhood.
  • Invent your animal: a blank fact card to create one that doesn't exist and pit it against the twelve.

WHY THIS ONE

Here kids color, read, look, write and play. And when the last drawing is colored in, the book is still useful: the cards can be played again and again.

Large A4 format (8.27 × 11.69 in) · 84 pages · full-color interior · ages 6 and up.
All you need: colored pencils and a pencil with an eraser. The back of every drawing is left blank.

The first book about Antarctica in the Selvativo collection. All the fact cards in the collection speak the same language, so these twelve can take on the animals from any other book. At the end, a sneak peek at the next one: Giants of the Ice.
