Cookie policy.

Last updated: October 22, 2026.

selvativo.com doesn't use cookies.

The only thing it stores in your browser

Every visit belongs to one of the eight books, and the whole website takes on the color of its world. So that the book doesn't change as you go from one page to another, your browser remembers which one you got: a single word, like “sabana” or “artico”.

  • It's kept in the browser's session storage (sessionStorage), not in a cookie.
  • It never leaves your device: it doesn't reach us or anyone else.
  • It's deleted on its own when you close the tab.
  • It's needed for the website to work the way you see it, so we don't need to ask your permission to store it.

What there isn't

No advertising, no visit counters, no social media buttons that follow you around. The fonts are served from the website itself, without asking Google for anything.

The websites we link to

Some buttons take you to Amazon, to buy the books, and to our Instagram. When you visit them, those websites may use their own cookies, under their own policies.

How to delete it

Close the tab. You can also delete it from your browser settings, under site data.

If this changes

If the website ever uses cookies or counts visits, we'll say so here before it happens, and we'll ask for your permission when needed. For any questions, write to contacto@selvativo.com. This English version is a translation; if there is any difference, the Spanish version prevails.
